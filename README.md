## Introduction

A group of scientifically-minded unicorns decided to put aside magic, and develop rocket technology capable of reaching space. Their crowning achievement so far has been the invention of a rocket engine powered completely by rainbow energy. These unicorns founded the "Unicorn Space Program", and set up a headquarters on a small planet where they could experiment in peace. 

The goal is simple: Launch rockets and complete missions.

Controls:

* Fire the rocket engine with "W" (or up arrow or bottom near the bottom right)
* Steer with "A" and "D" (or left/right arrow keys or buttons near bottom left)
* Reset with "R" (or button under "Other Controls")
* Start or go to next missions with "Enter"
* Zoom in or out with the mouse-wheel (or pinch on mobile)
* "Tab" will switch to continuous thrust (Tab again to switch back)
* Increase or decrease the throttle with "Shift" and "Control"
* "Z" gives max throttle, while "X" will cut it completely
* "O" toggles sound
* Just for fun: "B" breaks your rocket, and "F" attempts to fix it
